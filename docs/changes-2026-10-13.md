## 2. Web solver (`web/`)

A static page that solves boards in the browser, with every shortest solution of up to
10 moves: a board editor (paint start and goal, the 2x2 piece, the game's 100 boards as
presets, share links), the solver in Web Workers, and a step-by-step replay.

- `src/solver/web/web_solver.hpp` is a new, small solver: plain C++ (WebAssembly has no
  BMI2 and the page should not need cross-origin isolation for threads), compiled by
  `web/build_wasm.ps1` (Emscripten 4.0.23, one 85 KB JS file with the wasm embedded).
- Search: exact layers S_0..S_4 and G_0..G_4; up to 8 moves the layers meet, 9 and 10 moves
  stream each S_4 state 1 or 2 moves into G_4. Each worker owns a hash partition of S_4, so
  workers share nothing, and their solution lists concatenate. The rotation lane bound prunes
  the stream on normal boards.
- Checked by `testWebSolver` against `build/levels_final`: all 45 puzzles of 10 moves or fewer
  give the identical solution set (normal and 2x2, also split into 3 partitions).
- Speed in Chrome on this PC, with 18-1's 16-thread job running: up to 8 moves under ~2 s;
  8-1 (10 moves) 11 s on 12 workers; 6-5 (2x2, 10 moves) 19 s on 16 workers.
- Memory: the 4-move goal table is 8-90 MB per worker; the worker count is capped to a
  ~1.6 GB budget (less on low-memory devices).
