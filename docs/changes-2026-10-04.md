## 4. SSD depth-6 pipeline (`src/programs/experimentDiskDepth6.cpp`)

Splits `<S,3,6>`: depth-6 right table on X: in 256 partitions keyed by
`mixKey(StateHash)` (a separate mixer is needed so partitions are balanced).
`--seed-depth 3` = 12 moves, `4` = 13 moves.

| Step | 6-2 at 12 moves | Commit |
|---|---|---|
| Batched on-disk probe | 92 s | a796183 / 4d7a4d9 |
| u32 fingerprint index, single pass | 51.7 s | deab8b8 |
| Split-block Bloom filter (10 bits/state) | 33.4 s | 7540e8a |
| Compact per-partition index (u16 fp + u32 offsets, ~4.25 B/state), RAM-bounded passes (`--ram-gb`) | 23.0 s | 27c7796 |
| Large pages (`VirtualAlloc(MEM_LARGE_PAGES)`, needs "Lock pages in memory"; falls back) | 10.2 s warm / 22 s cold | 87c03fe |

9-3 at 13 moves: 37.8 min (two passes) → **21.4 min** (one pass), exact,
13 solutions. Probe lookups (~2.7 ns each) are ~80% of the time.

Other flags: `--rebuild`, `--no-recover`, `--batched`, `--no-large-pages`,
`--solutions file` (validates middles and the solution set), `--d7-sample N`.

## 5. Measured and rejected (not committed — don't retry naively)

- **Fully mixed `StateHash`**: in-RAM probe 2.7× slower (11-1 310 s → 578 s,
  probe CPU 2787 → 7516 s). The original xor/shift hash keeps similar boards
  close, so consecutive left probes hit nearby cache lines. Keep it.
- **Radix / address-sorted probing** in the SSD pipeline: 4.4× slower than
  the group-of-32 prefetch probe (stream+probe 9.5 s → 41 s).
- **Depth-7 table**: measured with `--d7-sample`: ~606 GB, ~12.6 h build on
  4 cores, and no gain at 13 moves.
- **Chunk size** of the left stream: no measurable effect.
