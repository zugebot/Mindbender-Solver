Mind Bender Solver

  1. Paint both boards, or pick one of the game's 100 puzzles at the top.
  2. Solve. You get every shortest solution up to 10 moves, and proof that none is shorter.
  3. Replay any solution move by move.

Runs on your device in WebAssembly; nothing is uploaded. Up to 8 moves takes about a second, 10 moves from seconds to a minute or two.

The boards have changed since this solve.

    How it works

    Notation

    R14
    row 1 shifted right 4
    C32
    column 3 shifted down 2
    R453
    the 2x2 piece's rows 4 and 5, right 3
    C231
    the 2x2 piece's columns 2 and 3, down 1

    Rows count from the top, columns from the left.

    Editing

    Click or drag to paint. Right-click a cell to pick its colour. Keys 1-8 choose a colour, M moves the 2x2 piece, Ctrl+Z undoes. The link button copies a link to the puzzle.

    The search

    The solver builds every board within 4 moves of the start and of the goal, and joins the two in the middle. The work is split over several threads, and a lane bound cuts away boards that can't reach the goal in time. It checks every move sequence, so "none shorter" is exact. It stops at 10 moves; longer puzzles need the desktop solver.
